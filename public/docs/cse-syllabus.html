Internal Assessment: 30 Marks (Mid-semester tests, quizzes, and continuous evaluation).
End-Semester Examination: 70 Marks conducted by JUT Ranchi.
Attendance Requirement: Mandatory 75% presence verified through Dumka Engineering College geofenced attendance system.
